Museum Art – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Who we are

Museum Art is developed by Berat Seyhan, an independent developer in Türkiye (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, write to beratseyhan@icloud.com.

In short

Museum Art has no account or sign-in, and we do not operate servers that collect your data. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads from Google AdMob; Google collects some data to deliver and measure these ads, as described below.

Data on your device

Your progress, settings and language choice are saved in a file on your device. This data is not sent to us and is deleted when you delete the app.

Advertising by Google AdMob

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK. When an ad is requested, Google may collect your IP address (used to estimate your approximate location), device identifiers (including Apple’s advertising identifier, IDFA, only if you allow tracking), information about the ads you see and tap, app interactions such as launches and taps, and diagnostic data such as crash and performance information. Google uses this data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalize ads. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

Your choices

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), the game asks for your consent with Google’s consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the Privacy Settings button in the game’s Settings. On iPhone and iPad the game asks for permission before it uses the advertising identifier; you can change this at any time in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. If you say no, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

Purchases

If the game offers in-app purchases, payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never receive your payment details.

Children

Museum Art is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children under 13. Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Türkiye’s personal data protection law, KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its processing. We do not hold personal data about you; for data processed by Google for advertising, use the choices above or see Google’s privacy policy. You can always contact us at beratseyhan@icloud.com.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the current version. If a translation differs from the English text, the English version applies.
